Act 1 — Clinical Context Defining the Disease Spectrum
MASLD Pathology Progression

Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease (MASLD) affects 10–20% of normal-BMI adults. Fat accumulation (steatosis) triggers cellular stress, insulin resistance, and inflammatory pathways.

Tissue Insulin Resistance (IR)

Pathological resistance to insulin action. Clinically estimated using fasting glucose and insulin levels:

HOMA-IR = (Glucose [mg/dL] × Insulin [μIU/mL]) / 22.5
FibroScan CAP (Reference Standard)

Controlled Attenuation Parameter from Transient Elastography quantifies liver fat in dB/m. CAP ≥ 248 dB/m indicates clinically significant hepatic steatosis.

Subcutaneous vs. Visceral Fat Distribution

BMI calculates total mass over height. It cannot differentiate between metabolic subcutaneous fat and dangerous visceral/ectopic fat stored around key organs.

The Ectopic Lipid Problem

Normal-BMI steatosis operates via distinct biological pathways: high carbohydrate intake, visceral fat mobilization, and liver de novo lipogenesis, bypassing traditional obesity markers.

Act 8 — Research Review Key Contributions & Honest Limitations
KEY CONTRIBUTIONS
• First normal-BMI screening focus addressing index collapse & inversion. • DA-SS-iVAE design delivering identifiable latent variables. • Mondrian subgroup calibration enforcing coverage safety (≥90.4%). • Symbolic regression formulas providing closed-form auditable logic. • Tested research prototype showcasing full-stack pipeline execution.
CLINICAL LIMITATIONS
• Cross-sectional data only (NHANES); causal direction cannot be established. • Single-cohort validation; external care assays pending verification. • Prevalence estimates carry high CI uncertainty range. • Proof-of-concept pipeline, not a cleared medical diagnostic device.
