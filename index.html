Clinical Background
Defining the Disease Space
MASLD — Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease
Hepatic steatosis (≥ 5% fat accumulation) in absence of excess alcohol, coupled with ≥1 metabolic risk factor. Formerly NAFLD. Spectrum: steatosis → MASH → fibrosis → cirrhosis. Affects ~10–20% of normal-BMI adults via ectopic lipid deposition ("lean MASLD").
Insulin Resistance (IR)
Pathological failure of cells to respond normally to insulin. Clinically approximated by HOMA-IR:
HOMA-IR = (fasting glucose × fasting insulin) / 22.5
FibroScan CAP — Reference Standard
Controlled Attenuation Parameter from Vibration-Controlled Transient Elastography (VCTE). Non-invasive liver fat quantification in dB/m. CAP ≥ 248 dB/m = clinically significant steatosis. Used as anchor supervision in LMSIS.
Lean MASLD — The Hidden Phenotype
In normal-BMI adults, steatosis follows non-obese pathways: ectopic visceral lipid deposition, insulin-mediated de novo lipogenesis. BMI cannot detect this — it measures total mass, not fat distribution.

Act 2 — Problem Definition
Problem Statement & Research Questions
P1 · DISCRIMINATIVE SIGNAL LOSS
BMI-Dependent Index Collapse
HSI's BMI term contributes ~45.6% of discriminative variance in mixed-BMI cohorts. In normal-BMI (σ ≈ 1.4 kg/m²), DCR of BMI collapses to ~5.6% — near-random (ρ = 0.092 OOD).
P2 · RISK RANKING INVERSION
MetS-Dependent Index Failure
NAFLD-LFS uses MetS criteria that correlate positively with liver fat in obese cohorts but invert in lean steatosis via non-MetS pathways. Result: ρ = −0.051 OOD — ranking sick patients as healthier.
P3 · LATENT NON-IDENTIFIABILITY
Standard VAE Axis Ambiguity
Any orthogonal rotation of standard VAE latent axes preserves the ELBO unchanged. Latent coordinates are biologically uninterpretable — no axis can be assigned a clinical meaning.
P4 · SUBGROUP COVERAGE FAILURE
Marginal Conformal Miscoverage
Standard marginal calibration achieves only 81.6% coverage for the Dual-Burden subgroup (target ≥90%). High-risk patients receive systematically under-protective uncertainty intervals.
  • RQ1: Can a formally anchored 2D latent metabolic representation be recovered from 14 routine biomarkers in normal-BMI adults?
  • RQ2: Do established clinical indices degrade or invert in this sub-population, and can this be explained structurally?
  • RQ3: Can Mondrian conformal prediction restore ≥90% subgroup coverage where marginal calibration fails?
  • RQ4: Can symbolic regression extract testable closed-form hypotheses from the neural decoder?

Act 2 — Objectives & Scope
Research Objectives & Scope
  • 1
    Design and implement a DA-SS-iVAE according to theoretical identifiability conditions (up to coordinate permutation and scaling) with demographic-conditioned priors and dual monotone anchor networks aligned to HOMA-IR and CAP.
  • 2
    Implement Mondrian conformal prediction stratified by phenotypic quadrant, restoring subgroup-conditional coverage guarantees (≥90%) for high-risk subpopulations.
  • 3
    Extract closed-form equations from the decoder via symbolic regression (PySR) to enable hypothesis generation about biomarker–latent relationships.
  • 4
    Develop a computational method for optimal path planning on the learned Riemannian manifold via Euler-Lagrange geodesic solving.
  • 5
    Engineer a full-stack research prototype (FastAPI backend, React Research Translation Layer) with integration testing and reproducible pipelines.

Act 8 — Contributions & Limitations
Key Contributions & Honest Limitations
RESEARCH CONTRIBUTIONS
1
Normal-BMI Specific Modelling Pipeline — custom screening architecture explicitly for 18.5–24.9 kg/m² adults, addressing discriminative collapse and risk-inversion failures.
2
DA-SS-iVAE Architecture — iVAE identifiability conditions (Khemakhem et al. 2020), demographic-conditioned priors, dual monotone anchors → approximately identifiable latent coordinates.
3
Mondrian Subgroup Calibration — first application of Mondrian conformal prediction to metabolic phenotyping; restores ≥90% coverage for high-risk subpopulation.
4
Symbolic Regression Interpretability — PySR-extracted closed-form decoder approximations; AIP product structure suggests super-additive Dual-Burden cardiovascular risk hypothesis.
5
Full-Stack Research Prototype — PyTorch model, FastAPI backend, React dashboard, 15-test integration suite, public repository — engineering maturity uncommon in student research.
HONEST LIMITATIONS
Cross-sectional design only. No temporal trajectory. Causal ordering between IR and steatosis development cannot be established.
Single survey source. All data from NHANES. External validity to non-US populations, different assay protocols, or primary care settings unconfirmed.
Prevalence estimate is exploratory. 29.89% Dual-Burden estimate carries 95% CI of [0.00M, 64.36M]. The point estimate must not be cited without the interval.
Asian threshold is pilot-grade. n=12 reference band finding superseded by full cohort (n=355). Real KNHANES validation still required.
Not a diagnostic device. Research prototype only. No FDA/CE clearance. Must not direct patient care.

Act 8 — Future Work
Eight Directions Forward
  • Independent external validation on a non-NHANES cohort with different demographics, lab assays, and collection protocols — the single most critical gap
  • Prospective outcome tracking — does Dual-Burden assignment predict MASLD progression, T2D incidence, or major adverse cardiovascular events?
  • Expanded ancestral equity cohorts — validate the differential HOMA-IR threshold finding (currently n=12 pilot) in enriched Asian-ancestry datasets including real KNHANES data
  • Missing-data robustness — partial-input inference or multiple imputation when not all 14 biomarkers are co-ordered in primary care
  • Deep tabular baselines + longitudinal extension — compare against TabNet, FT-Transformer; extend to recurrent or state-space architectures for trajectory modelling
  • Clinician usability study — formal evaluation of interpretability, trust, and clinical utility of the latent-space visualisation with primary care physicians
  • Regulatory & safety audit — adversarial robustness, fairness auditing, EHR integration design, and FDA/CE pathway scoping before any clinical deployment
