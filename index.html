"Predictive Risk Intelligence for Metabolic Screening in Diabetes"

"LMSIS — Latent Metabolic State Inference System"

"A deep generative model that recovers hidden metabolic risk from routine blood biomarkers in adults with a normal body weight."

"View on GitHub" "Read the Full Report" "Try the Live Demo"

"Millions of adults with a clinically normal body weight may carry hidden, concurrent insulin resistance and hepatic steatosis — a combination of metabolic dysfunction that routine screening, built around Body Mass Index, is structurally unable to detect. LMSIS is a semi-supervised deep generative model that learns a two-dimensional metabolic coordinate space directly from fourteen routine blood biomarkers, with each axis mathematically anchored to a real clinical reference measurement: insulin resistance and liver fat content. The system places every patient on this map, provides a calibrated confidence range specific to their risk group, and computes a route back toward a metabolically healthy position — translated into the exact biomarker changes that would move them there."

"+ Technical details"

"The architecture is a Dual-Anchored Semi-Supervised Identifiable Variational Autoencoder (DA-SS-iVAE). Identifiability follows from conditioning the latent prior on demographic covariates, per Khemakhem et al. (2020). Each anchor network is constrained to strictly positive weights via a Softplus reprojection, guaranteeing monotonicity between each coordinate and its clinical target. The steatosis anchor is trained semi-supervised through a binary availability mask, since imaging-derived liver-fat labels are only present for a subset of participants. Per-subgroup uncertainty is provided by Mondrian conformal prediction, stratified by phenotypic quadrant, directly addressing the subgroup coverage impossibility result of Barber et al. (2023). Intervention pathways are computed as geodesics on the Riemannian manifold induced by the decoder Jacobian, solved numerically and projected back to biomarker space."
