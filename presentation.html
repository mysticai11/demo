The Blindspot: Silent Ectopic Fat

Standard BMI (18.5 - 24.9 kg/m²) measures mass, but is entirely blind to fat distribution. Beneath a "healthy" exterior, patients fall into 4 distinct phenotypes:

1. Metabolically Healthy (MHNW)

The baseline cohort. Low risk of cardiovascular progression or diabetes.

2. IR-Dominant (Z₁ Elevated)

Isolated tissue insulin resistance. These patients respond to sensitizers like Metformin.

3. Steatosis-Dominant (Z₂ Elevated)

Isolated hepatic lipid accumulation. This group requires Statins or Fibrates.

4. Dual-Burden (High Risk)

Both axes elevated. The highest-risk subpopulation carrying a severe, silent dual burden.

Anticipated Questions

Is this ready for clinical deployment?

No — it is a validated computational framework. Prospective clinical trials are required before diagnostic use.

Why an iVAE over a standard ResNet?

Because standard VAEs suffer from unidentifiability. Our dual-anchored formulation guarantees biologically interpretable latent dimensions.

Does the model overfit the NHANES dataset?

No — rigorous temporal OOD testing confirmed robust generalizability two years out.

What about the unlabelled data?

The semi-supervised architecture uses massive unlabeled lab sets to refine the manifold, vastly outperforming purely supervised baseline models.

Current Limitations

  • Reliance on a single national cross-sectional data source.
  • Lacks longitudinal tracking for individual disease progression.

Future Research Directions

  • Prospective clinical trials to measure real-world diagnostic impact.
  • Integration of time-series modeling for temporal risk trajectories.
  • Expansion of the validation cohort to highly heterogeneous populations.
